How to Play How Well Do You Know Mommy (Free Printable)

How Well Do You Know Mommy is the baby shower game where guests test how closely they know the mom-to-be. Print one free question sheet per guest, then have everyone write down their best guesses about mommy — her favorite snack, her due date, how many kids she wants, and more. When everyone is done, mommy reads out her real answers one by one. Guests score a point for each match, and whoever knows the mommy-to-be best wins. It is easy, personal, and gets the whole room laughing.

Printing & Setup Tips

Print the sheet on standard 8.5x11 paper — one copy per guest, plus one extra for mommy to fill in the correct answers. Black-and-white printing works perfectly and keeps costs down. Set out pens as guests arrive so they can start early, and print a few spares for last-minute additions. If you want a keepsake, collect the finished sheets afterward for mommy to look back on.

Fun Variations

  • Daddy's answers: Have dad-to-be secretly fill in a sheet too, then compare his guesses to mommy's real answers for extra laughs.
  • Team play: Split guests into teams and let each table answer together for a more relaxed, social version.
  • Prize round: Award a small gift to the top scorer and a booby prize to whoever knows mommy least well.
  • Baby edition: Add questions about the baby — chosen name, nursery theme, or who the baby will look like — for a forward-looking twist.

How Well Do You Know Mommy FAQ

Is this how well do you know mommy game free to print?

Yes. This printable how well do you know mommy baby shower game is completely free — just print as many question sheets as you have guests, no sign-up required.

What questions are on the mommy-to-be sheet?

The sheet includes fun questions about the mommy-to-be such as her favorite food, her due date, her cravings, how she is feeling, and her hopes for the baby. You can play exactly as printed or add your own questions.

How many players can play?

Any number. Because each guest gets their own printable sheet, the game works just as well for a small gathering as it does for a large baby shower.

How do you score how well do you know mommy?

Mommy reads out her real answers after everyone has guessed. Each guest scores one point per matching answer, and the guest with the most points wins.
