How do I download and print the games?

Open any game, adjust the options if you'd like, and click the download button to get an instant PDF. Print it at home on standard letter-size paper, or send the PDF to a local print shop for color copies.

How many baby shower games should I plan?

For a two-hour baby shower, three to five games usually works well. Mix a quick icebreaker like bingo or emoji pictionary with a longer activity like word scramble or trivia to keep every guest engaged.
