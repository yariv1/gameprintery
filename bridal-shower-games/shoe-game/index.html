How to Play the Bridal Shower Shoe Game (Free Printable)

The Bridal Shower Shoe Game is the hilarious "who's more likely" game where the bride and her partner sit back-to-back, each holding one of their own shoes and one of their partner's. The host reads out questions like "Who said I love you first?" and each answer holds up the shoe of the person they think fits. When their raised shoes match, the room cheers — when they clash, everyone laughs. Print the free question list, grab two pairs of shoes, and you are ready to play.

Printing & Setup Tips

Print the question list on standard 8.5x11 paper — one copy for the host to read from. Seat the bride and partner back-to-back in the middle of the room so guests can see their reactions. Each person holds one of their own shoes and one of their partner's. Black-and-white printing works perfectly, and it helps to read through the questions in advance so the funniest ones land at the right moment.

Fun Variations

  • Funny questions: Mix in cheeky or embarrassing prompts about the couple to get the biggest laughs from guests.
  • For the couple keepsake: Film the round so the couple can rewatch their reactions after the wedding.
  • Guest guesses: Have guests predict each answer before the shoes go up, scoring a point when they guess right.
  • Add your own: Use the template to write custom questions about the couple's own story and inside jokes.
