How to Play Bridal Shower Word Scramble (Free Printable)

Bridal Shower Word Scramble is the quick puzzle game where each line shows a jumbled wedding-related word — like bouquet, honeymoon, or wedding — that guests race to unscramble. Print one free sheet per guest, hand out pens, and set a time limit. Whoever unscrambles the most words correctly wins. It is an easy icebreaker guests can start the moment they sit down, and it works for showers of any size.

Printing & Setup Tips

Print the word scramble sheet on standard 8.5x11 paper — one copy per guest — plus one answer sheet for the host. Black-and-white printing works perfectly and keeps costs low. Set out pens as guests arrive so they can start early, and print a few spares for late arrivals. Keep the answer sheet aside until time is up so no one peeks.

Fun Variations

  • Team play: Split guests into teams and let each table unscramble together for a more social version.
  • Speed round: Set a two-minute timer and see who can solve the most words before time runs out.
  • Prize round: Award a small gift to the fastest solver and a booby prize to whoever finishes last.
  • Make your own: Add scrambled words tied to the bride — her name, hometown, or hobbies — for a personal twist.
