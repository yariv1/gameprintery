How to Play Bridal Shower Bingo (Free Printable)

Bridal Shower Bingo is the classic party game where each guest gets a printable bingo card filled with squares to mark off as the bride opens her gifts. When the bride unwraps an item that matches a square — like lingerie, kitchenware, or a gift card — guests cross it off. The first guest to complete a row, column, or full card shouts "Bingo!" and wins. Print one free card per guest, hand out pens, and you are ready to play.

Printing & Setup Tips

Print the bingo cards on standard 8.5x11 paper — one card per guest — so everyone has their own unique board. Color printing looks great, but black-and-white works perfectly and keeps costs down. Set out pens as guests arrive so they can start as soon as the bride begins opening gifts, and print a few spare cards for late arrivals.

Fun Variations

  • Blackout round: Instead of a single line, require guests to fill the entire card to win for a longer game.
  • Prize table: Offer a small gift to the first winner and a runner-up prize to keep everyone playing.
  • Write-your-own: Have guests fill their own squares with gifts they predict the bride will receive.
  • Free-space twist: Add a themed center square, like "something handmade," that everyone marks at the start.
