How to Play Bachelorette Bingo (Free Printable)

Bachelorette Bingo is the easy icebreaker that keeps the whole party watching for the fun to unfold. Each guest gets a free printable bingo card filled with squares describing things that tend to happen on a bachelorette night — the bride putting on her sash, someone ordering shots, a stranger buying a round, or the group belting out karaoke. When a square happens in real life, players mark it off. The first guest to complete a row, column, or diagonal shouts "Bingo!" and wins. Print one card per guest, hand out pens, and let the night play itself.

Printing & Setup Tips

Print the bingo cards on standard 8.5x11 paper so every guest gets their own unique board — the generator reshuffles each card so no two match. Color printing looks fun and photographs well, but black-and-white works perfectly if you're printing on the go. Bring a few pens and a couple of spare printed cards for anyone who joins late, and keep the cards in your bag so they're ready the moment the party starts.

Fun Variations

  • Blackout round: Require guests to fill the entire card instead of one line for a game that lasts the whole night.
  • Prize for the winner: Offer a small gift — a mini bottle of bubbly or a face mask — to the first player to call bingo.
  • Dare squares: Turn a few squares into mini-dares the bride must complete before anyone can mark them.
  • Write-your-own: Let the maid of honor fill blank squares with inside jokes and predictions about the bride's big night.

Bachelorette Bingo FAQ

Are these bachelorette bingo cards free to print?

How many bingo cards can I print?

As many as you need. Each guest should get their own printable card, and the generator creates unique boards so no two players share the same squares.

What goes in the bachelorette bingo squares?

The squares describe fun moments that pop up on a bachelorette night out or party — from the bride's sash and matching outfits to toasts, dares, and late-night food runs — so guests mark them off as they happen.

How do you win bachelorette bingo?

The first guest to mark off a full row, column, or diagonal calls "Bingo!" and wins. For a longer game that lasts all night, play a blackout round where the whole card must be filled.
