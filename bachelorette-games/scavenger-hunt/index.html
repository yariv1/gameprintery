How to Play Bachelorette Scavenger Hunt (Free Printable)

Pick Clean or Spicy tasks, choose how many you want (10, 15, or 20), and hit Shuffle to build a fresh list. Turn points on for a competitive game or off for a plain checklist, then print one list per player. Whoever checks off the most tasks — or racks up the most points — before the night ends wins. It works just as well for a night out on the town as it does for an at-home party.

Printing & Setup Tips

Print or photocopy one checklist per player or per team so everyone can race at once. Assign one person as judge, agree that a phone photo counts as proof, and set a time limit to keep things moving. Keep a small prize on hand for the winner, and shuffle a new list if you want a fresh round.

Fun Variations

  • Team Hunt: Split into pairs and give each team the same printable list — first team to finish wins.
  • Points Battle: Keep points on and total the scores at the end instead of racing to finish.
  • Photo-Only Round: Require a picture for every task to settle disputes and save the memories.
  • Bride's Choice: Let the bride pick the count and the Clean or Spicy vibe for the whole crew.

Bachelorette Scavenger Hunt FAQ

Is this bachelorette scavenger hunt list free to print?

Yes. Build your list, hit Print, and you get a clean checklist to print at home — no email or sign-up needed.

What's the difference between the clean and spicy tasks?

Clean tasks are wholesome party dares anyone can do in public. Spicy tasks are flirty and cheeky but stay tasteful, so they're safe for a mixed crowd.

How many tasks should each player get?

Ten keeps a quick game moving, 15 is the sweet spot for a full night, and 20 makes a longer team challenge.

Can I use this as a template for my own ideas?

Absolutely — print a list as a starting point, then cross out or add your own ideas to fit the group and the venue.
