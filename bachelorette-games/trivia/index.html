How to Play Bachelorette Trivia (Free Printable)

Bachelorette Trivia is the quiz game where guests answer fun bachelorette trivia questions about the bride, the couple, and classic party-night traditions. Print one free question sheet per guest, hand out pens, and give everyone a few minutes to write their answers. When time is up, read the correct answers aloud — guests score a point for each one they got right, and the highest total wins. It is quick to set up, gets everyone laughing, and works whether you are at home, on a getaway, or out for the night.

Printing & Setup Tips

Print the question sheet on standard 8.5x11 paper — one copy per guest — plus one answer sheet for whoever is hosting. Black-and-white printing works perfectly and keeps costs low. Set out pens as guests arrive so they can start early, and print a few spares for last-minute additions. Keep the answer sheet aside until everyone has finished so no one peeks.

Fun Variations

  • About the bride: Swap in questions about the bride and how the couple met, their first date, or the proposal for a personal twist.
  • Team play: Split guests into teams and let each group answer together for a more social version.
  • Drink-if round: For a 21+ party, anyone who gets a question wrong takes a sip instead of losing a point.
  • Prize round: Award a small gift to the top scorer and a funny booby prize to whoever scores the lowest.
